About us

Homes only. Done right.

Go Roofline Lights is a locally owned installer based in Avondale. We light up houses and nothing else: no storefronts, no office parks. Every job is somebody's home, and we treat it that way.

We install Govee Permanent Outdoor Lights 2 in a slim aluminum track under your roofline. During the day you barely notice it. At night, it's whatever you want it to be.

FAQ

Questions people ask

How much does it cost?

Every roofline is different, so we give you an exact price after a free in-person measurement. Our prices are significantly lower than most permanent lighting companies in the Valley.

Do you do businesses or commercial buildings?

No. We only work on homes.

Can I change the colors?

Yes, from the free app, anytime. Pick a solid color, a pattern, or set a schedule so the lights change on their own.

Do they stay up all year?

Yes. The lights sit in a low-profile track under the roofline. They're built for the outdoors and stay put through Arizona summers and monsoon season.

How long does install take?

Usually one day. We measure ahead of time, so on install day we show up with everything we need.

Do I need an outdoor outlet?

Yes, installs need an existing working outdoor outlet near the lights. We'll check it during your free measurement.

What lights do you install?

Govee Permanent Outdoor Lights 2, mounted in an aluminum track. They're controlled from the free Govee Home app.

What areas do you serve?

Avondale, Goodyear, and Litchfield Park. If you're nearby, ask us. We may be able to help.
