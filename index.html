Questions people ask

Can I change the colors?

Yes, from the free app, anytime. Pick a solid color, a pattern, or set a schedule so the lights change on their own.

Do they stay up all year?

Yes, the lights sit in a low-profile track under the roofline. They're built for the outdoors and stay put through Arizona summers and monsoon season.

How long does install take?

Usually one day. We measure ahead of time, so on install day we show up with everything we need.

Do I need an outdoor outlet?

Yes, installs require an existing working outdoor outlet near the lights. We'll check it during your free measurement.

What areas do you serve?
