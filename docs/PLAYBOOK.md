# The Playbook — how to actually get this agent buyers

The software is the easy part. This is the part that makes money.

---

## 1. Understand what you are selling

Daniel's advantage is not that he sells houses. Hundreds of agents in Phoenix sell
houses. His advantage is:

1. **He speaks Spanish**, and works the West Valley — Avondale, Goodyear, Buckeye,
   Tolleson, Glendale, Laveen — where a large share of buyers prefer Spanish.
2. **He does ITIN loans.** Most families with an ITIN and no Social Security number
   believe home ownership is closed to them. It is not, and almost nobody is telling
   them clearly.

That second point is the entire business. Every high-performing post on his Page is
an ITIN post. Lead with it.

**The one message that works:**

> "Con tu número ITIN sí puedes comprar casa. Desde 5% de enganche.
> No se requiere Seguro Social."

## 2. Where the buyers actually are

Ranked by what returns per hour of effort:

| Rank | Channel | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Comments on his own posts** | They already raised their hand. Highest conversion by a wide margin. |
| 2 | **Local Spanish-language Facebook groups** | "Avondale AZ Compra y Venta", "Mexicanos en Phoenix", church and school groups |
| 3 | **Facebook Marketplace rentals** | Everyone browsing rentals is a buyer who does not know it yet |
| 4 | **Referrals from closed clients** | One happy ITIN buyer tells their whole extended family |
| 5 | **Craigslist "housing wanted"** | Low volume, but people there are searching right now |
| 6 | **Paid Lead Ads** | Works, but only after the organic channels are running |

### Groups worth joining

Search Facebook for these, in this order:

```
Avondale AZ compra y venta          Goodyear AZ community
Mexicanos en Phoenix Arizona        West Valley AZ families
Casas en renta Phoenix              Buckeye AZ neighbors
Maricopa AZ community               Latinos en Arizona
Empleos Phoenix AZ                  Yard sale / venta de garage groups (West Valley)
```

Rental groups outperform buying groups. People looking to rent are one good
conversation away from realising they could own.

### Group rules

Most ban direct promotion, and the ban is enforced. What works:

- **Never open with a pitch.** Answer the question that was asked, completely, for free.
- Ask the moderator before posting anything promotional. Many will say yes to one
  educational post a month.
- Answer *every* comment on your own posts, even "🔥". The algorithm rewards it and
  it is how DMs start.

## 3. The comment-to-client pipeline

This is the core loop. It is why the "Paste FB comments" tool exists.

```
Post about ITIN  →  people comment "info" / "cuánto?"  →  reply publicly
        ↓
   DM them privately  →  ask 3 questions  →  add to pipeline
        ↓
   Hand to Daniel  →  he calls  →  showings  →  closing  →  you get paid
```

**Public reply** (builds trust, everyone else reading sees it):

> "¡Claro que sí! Te mando la información por mensaje ahorita. 📩"

**Then DM** — three questions, no more:

> 1. ¿En qué ciudad te gustaría comprar?
> 2. ¿Cuánto tienes ahorrado para el enganche?
> 3. ¿Ya hablaste con un prestamista?

Those three answers give you a lead score of 60+ and tell Daniel everything he needs
for the first call.

**Speed matters more than polish.** A lead contacted in 5 minutes converts at several
times the rate of one contacted the next day. Score 70+ means call today.

## 4. What to post

Use the Content tab. The weekly rhythm:

| Day | Post | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Market update | A number people react to |
| Tue | Listing | Shows inventory, builds credibility |
| Wed | **ITIN program** | Your highest-performing post — never skip |
| Thu | Buyer tip | Education; invites questions |
| Fri | Open house | Weekend traffic |
| Sat | **Question post** | Highest comment volume of the week |
| Sun | Testimonial | Trust for the week ahead |

Best times, Phoenix: **7:30am · 12:15pm · 6:30pm · 8:30pm**

Question posts are the secret weapon. "¿En qué ciudad comprarías?" with three options
generates dozens of comments — and every comment is a lead you can paste in.

## 5. The money

Standard referral agreement:

```
Sale price       $400,000
Agent commission   × 2.5%   = $10,000
Your referral      ×  25%   =  $2,500
```

Realistic pace once the system is running:

| | Leads/mo | Closings/mo | Your monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1-2 (building) | 20-40 | 0 | $0 |
| Month 3-4 | 40-60 | 1 | ~$2,500 |
| Month 6+ | 60-100 | 2-3 | $5,000-$7,500 |

Real estate has a long delay — a lead captured today closes in 60 to 120 days. The
first two months will look like nothing is working. **Pipeline value** on the Revenue
tab is the number to watch during that stretch, not Earned.

### Get the agreement in writing first

Before sending a single lead, agree with Daniel in writing on:

- The referral percentage (25% is standard)
- When it is paid (normally at closing, from his commission)
- What counts as "your" lead, and how long that claim lasts (90-180 days is typical)
- How disputes get settled

**A note on licensing:** Arizona restricts who may be paid a fee for real estate
activity. Unlicensed referral arrangements are legally limited, and the rules differ
depending on whether you are a licensee, an unlicensed referrer, or simply
introducing people. Confirm your specific arrangement with Daniel's designated
broker or the Arizona Department of Real Estate before money changes hands. Getting
this right at the start protects both of you.

## 6. Rules to follow

- **TCPA** — get consent before texting or calling. The landing page captures it;
  keep it that way. Honor opt-outs immediately ("Mark do-not-contact" in the lead drawer).
- **Fair Housing** — never target or exclude by race, religion, national origin,
  family status, disability or sex. Advertise the *program* (ITIN loans), never a
  demographic. "Programas con ITIN disponibles" is fine. Targeting an ethnicity is not.
- **Meta's terms** — do not scrape, do not automate the browser, do not run fake
  accounts. The Page is the asset; protect it.
- **Advertising** — Arizona requires the brokerage name on real estate advertising.
  Add it in Settings so it appears on the landing page.

## 7. First week

- [ ] **Day 1** — `npm run seed`, look around, then `npm run reset`. Set your commission split in Settings. Get the referral agreement in writing.
- [ ] **Day 2** — Build the prospecting worklist. Join 5-10 local groups. Read their rules.
- [ ] **Day 3** — Generate and post the ITIN post. Answer every comment.
- [ ] **Day 4** — Paste yesterday's comments into the importer. DM everyone.
- [ ] **Day 5** — Call every lead scoring 70+. Hand the qualified ones to Daniel.
- [ ] **Day 6** — Post a question post. Expect the most comments of the week.
- [ ] **Day 7** — Review the Revenue tab. Note which source produced the best leads, and do more of that.

Then repeat. The compounding is real: month 3 looks nothing like month 1.
